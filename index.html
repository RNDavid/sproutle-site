Sproutle

Seeds to blooms: a relaxing merge puzzle garden

Swipe to slide every tile on the board. When two matching tiles meet, they grow into the next stage: seed, sprout, bud, flower and on to bigger blooms.

  • Short, calm levels, every one proven solvable
  • Endless levels, the same for every player
  • Thaw frosted tiles and fill harvest baskets
  • Grow a garden, one plant per level
  • Plays offline: no timers, no lives
