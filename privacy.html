Sproutle Privacy Policy

Effective date: 10/31/2026 · Sproutle by Reveille Entertainment

In short:

  • Sproutle has no accounts and does not ask for your name, email or any other personal details.
  • Your progress and settings are stored only on your device.
  • Sproutle shows ads from Google AdMob. Google collects some device information to show ads and measure them, as described below.
  • Where the law requires it (for example in the European Economic Area and the UK), you are asked for consent before ads are personalised, and you can change your choice at any time in Settings.

Information Sproutle itself handles

Sproutle saves your level progress, your best stars per level and your settings (such as whether vibration is on) in the app's private storage on your device. This information never leaves your device: it is not sent to us or to anyone else. Uninstalling the app or clearing its storage deletes it.

Sproutle does not use analytics services, does not create accounts, and does not collect your name, email address, contacts, photos or precise location.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

Sproutle is free and supported by ads served by Google AdMob, which is provided by Google. To show and measure ads and to prevent fraud, Google's software in the app may collect and process information such as:

  • your device's advertising ID or app set ID and other device identifiers;
  • your IP address, which can indicate your approximate location;
  • information about your device, such as its model, operating system and language;
  • how you interact with ads, such as which ads are shown or tapped;
  • diagnostic information about ad performance.

Google may use this information as described in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services and the Google Privacy Policy. We do not receive this information in a form that identifies you.

Ads may be shown when you choose to watch one (for example, to undo a move or to keep playing after running out of moves), between levels, and on the title screen. Ads are not shown on the game board while you play.

Your choices

  • Consent: where required by law, Sproutle shows Google's consent message before ads are personalised. You can review or change your choice at any time with Settings → Privacy choices in the app.
  • Personalised ads: on Android you can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of personalised ads, in your device settings (usually Settings → Google → Ads or Settings → Privacy → Ads).
  • Your data held by Google: requests about data Google holds can be made through Google's own tools, described in the Google Privacy Policy.

Children

While we want everyone to play, Sproutle is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Permissions

Sproutle asks Android for internet access, used only to load ads, and for vibration, used for short haptic feedback during play (which you can turn off in Settings).

Security and retention

Information Sproutle stores stays on your device until you uninstall the app or clear its data. Information collected by Google is kept and protected according to Google's policies.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
